Your theatre list,

How it works

  1. Enter the list in one of three ways: upload the calendar PDF, upload a Word document, or type it in. Each booking becomes a case, in theatre order.
  2. Review the cases. Amber Check marks are values the app worked out for you.
  3. Export the hospital list as Excel, or print it.

Patient details stay on this device. They are never saved or sent anywhere.

Enter the OT list

Choose one of the three ways below.

  1. In Google Calendar, open the theatre day in Day view.
  2. Print it and choose Save as PDF.
  3. Tap the box below and choose that PDF.
  1. Write the list in Word, laid out as in the example below.
  2. Save it as a .docx file. For an older .doc file, open it in Word and use Save As → Word Document (.docx).
  3. Tap the box below and choose that file.
Tap to choose the PDF or drag it here

Type each booking as shown in the example below, or paste text copied from another document or message.

How each booking must be written

Each booking needs these, in this order:

  1. Patient name
  2. Age/Sex, e.g. 45/F or 70 M. This is required: entries without it are skipped.
  3. Procedure, e.g. Lap chole, Ex bx lipoma back, RIH mesh repair
  4. Anaesthesia (optional): LA, GA or LA/Sed
  5. Reg #, e.g. Reg # 123456, or Reg # NYR if not yet registered
  6. Time slot, e.g. 8am - 9am or 9:30am to 11:30am, on the next line or at the start of the line. The slot length becomes EST (MIN).

Example

Mary Brown 45/F Ex bx lipoma back LA Reg # 123456
8am - 9am
Ann White 38/F Lap chole GA Reg # 445566
9:30am - 11:30am

This also works

8am - 9am  Mary Brown 45/F Ex bx lipoma back LA Reg # 123456
9:30am - 11:30am  Ann White 38/F Lap chole GA Reg # 445566

Put the date (e.g. Tue Nov 17, 2026) and site (e.g. FAL OT) anywhere in the text and they're filled in for you. Otherwise set them below.
