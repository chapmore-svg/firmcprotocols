How it works

  1. Tap New colonoscopy or New upper GI.
  2. Work down the sections: tap chips, step the sedation doses, add findings segment by segment, import photos.
  3. Preview, then Save PDF (straight into the patient's folder on a computer, once set up in Settings) or Share it (Gmail, WhatsApp, Drive…).

Reports are saved as you type, on this device only, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it. They clear themselves after 30 days (change this in Settings). Keep the device screen-locked.
