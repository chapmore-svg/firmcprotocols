How it works

  1. Choose a document and fill it in. It's saved as a draft on this device as you type.
  2. Tap Preview. Sign with your finger or mouse, or use your saved signature.
  3. Save PDF (into the patient's folder on a computer, once set up in Settings), Share (Gmail, WhatsApp, Drive…) or Print.

Your details, signature and drafts stay on this device; nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it. Keep the device screen-locked.
