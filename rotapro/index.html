The month's rota,
built and checked.

First, add your team. Enter the names of your interns, SHO, MOs and consultants once. Rota Pro remembers them on this device.

How it works

  1. Setup: choose the month and the duty cycle. Last month's edits on the shared week carry over.
  2. Rota: Rota Pro shares out the days off. Tap any day for leave, DOH, overtime, covers, holidays or staff changes. Import the overtime spreadsheet.
  3. Check: the audit confirms the duty cycle, days off and weekend pairs.
  4. Export: Excel, PDF, print, or share to WhatsApp, Gmail and Drive.

Rotas and names are kept on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it.
